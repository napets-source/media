Welcome to the world of Generation X.

Born between approximately 1965 and 1980, Generation X grew up during a time of major technological change.

From cassette players and landline telephones to the first personal computers and video game consoles, technology was beginning to change everyday life.

Unlike teenagers today, communication was not instant, entertainment was often physical, and privacy meant something very different.

Explore the technology, culture and everyday experiences of a teenager growing up in the 1980s, and discover just how different life was before the digital age.
